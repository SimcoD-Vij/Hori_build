🏛️ Regulatory Agent

Not investigating fraud — watching whether the system itself is operating legally.

Plain English: This agent doesn't look at individual suspects — it looks at whether our entire investigation system is following the rules. Think of it as an internal compliance officer that never sleeps. It checks: Has the technical documentation been kept up to date? Did a real human review each case (not just click-through)? Are we about to miss a legal deadline for filing a suspicious activity report? Has the red-team probe run recently enough to satisfy the EU AI Act's continuous risk monitoring requirement?

LangGraph Role

The Regulatory Agent is the final node after Verification PASS. It runs per-case compliance checks before the case is committed to the Audit Log. Its result (compliance_result: COMPLIANT | NON_COMPLIANT) is recorded in the case state but does not reopen the fraud investigation — it creates a compliance record for a compliance officer to review separately.
