🔴 Red-Team Agent

Deliberately trying to beat our own detection system — and recording what it finds.

Plain English: Imagine a security team that knows all your alarm codes and tries to rob the building. If they succeed, you know you have a problem. That is what this agent does — it generates fake transactions designed to look suspicious but avoid triggering our detection rules. If it finds a way to sneak through, it logs a "blind spot" and recommends a rule fix. Its results also feed the Regulatory Agent as evidence of the continuous risk management process required by the EU AI Act.

EU AI Act Connection

The Red-Team Agent's ongoing probe loop satisfies EU AI Act Article 9's requirement for a continuous, iterative risk management system. Each result is appended to data/redteam_history.jsonl and updates data/system_card.json with the last probe date. The Regulatory Agent reads this to verify the required cadence is being maintained.
